## Description

Forge on-brand generative patterns in one click, as fully editable Figma layers.

Pattern Forge builds layered compositions from colour blocks, multiply overlaps, a gradient block, halftone dots and small serif data-labels. Everything lands as native Figma nodes. Nothing is an image, and nothing is flattened, so you can recolour, move or retype any piece afterwards.

**Four ways to forge**

• Random: nothing selected? Get a fresh composition at any output size.
• Fill Selection: select any shape, even a star or a circle, and the pattern follows its real outline.
• Anchor Around Selection: your shape stays the focal point while the pattern builds around it.
• Batch Selection: select several frames and fill them all as one family.

**What you control**

• Density, grid strictness and block size range
• An 11-colour brand palette. Turn colours on or off, or swap in your own hex values
• Blend modes: Multiply, Screen, Darken or Normal
• Halftone texture and the gradient block, each on or off
• Data-readout labels, with your own text and font
• Output size: Square, Widescreen, Portrait, Social, or custom
• Seed. The same seed and settings always give the same pattern

**Made for real work**

• Live editing: select a forged frame and every change updates it in place
• Three built-in presets (Dense Cluster, Scattered Minimal, High Contrast Mono-Block), plus your own saved presets
• Works offline. No account, no network access, no tracking

Great for brand systems, posters, social posts, website heroes, slides and packaging.

Free and open source: https://github.com/nurfarhad/pattern-forge
